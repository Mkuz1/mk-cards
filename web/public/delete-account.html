Delete your account and data

Delete it yourself, straight away

Deleting your account removes everything at once. It cannot be undone.

In the MK Cards app (iPhone or Android): open the Account tab, tap Delete account, and confirm with your password (or your username if you sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft).

On the website: sign in, open Account, and choose Delete my account.

Sign in and delete my account

Can't sign in? Ask us to delete it

Send us a request from the email address on your account and include your MK Cards username. We confirm by email and delete your account and data within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

Or write to support.

What is deleted

Your account (username, email address, password and any Apple, Google or Microsoft sign-in links), all your cards and their photos and logos, your card links and QR codes (they stop working), the contacts you collected or scanned, your card statistics, and any Salesforce connection you set up.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Records of failed sign-in attempts are kept for up to 24 hours to prevent abuse, then removed automatically. Wallet passes already saved on other people's phones and contacts other people saved from your card stay on their devices, outside our control.

Company accounts: if you are a member of a company workspace, your company's administrator can also remove you. The owner of a workspace with other members must hand ownership over before deleting their account.
