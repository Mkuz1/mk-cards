Common questions

How do I share my card? Open the Share tab (or Share on the website) to show your QR code, copy your link, add the card to Apple or Google Wallet, or write it to an NFC tag.

I forgot my password. Passwords are never reset by email. Sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft if you connected one, or ask your company administrator to reset it. Contact us if you are stuck.

How do I scan a business card? Open Scan, choose Business card, and take a photo. Check the details, then save. Badges with a QR code can be scanned the same way.

How do I connect Salesforce? Company administrators: Admin › Salesforce › Connect. Individuals: Contacts › Salesforce.

Someone left our company. On the Admin page, open the person and choose "Left the company". Their card shows your company's message instead of their details.

How do I delete my account? Account › Delete my account. Everything is removed from our servers. Can't sign in? Request deletion here.
