Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

MK Cards lets you create digital business cards, share them with a link, QR code, NFC tag or wallet pass, and keep the contacts of people you meet. This policy explains what we store and why.

What we store

Your account: username, email address and a scrambled form of your password that cannot be read back. If you sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft, we store the identifier that service gives us and the email address it shares.

Your cards: the details you choose to add, such as name, job title, company, phone numbers, email addresses, WhatsApp number, website, address, links, photo and logo.

Contacts you collect: details visitors choose to send you from your card page, and details of business cards and badges you scan or type in.

Usage of your cards: counts of how often each card is viewed, saved or tapped, by day. We do not record who the visitors are.

Security records: for up to a day, the internet address of sign-up and failed sign-in attempts, to prevent abuse.

Scanning cards

When you scan a business card, the photo is sent to our server and read by an AI model run by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, to fill in the contact's details. The photo is not stored. In the iPhone app, the text is first read on your phone.

Who can see it

Anyone with a card's link or QR code can see that card. Contacts you collect are visible only to you and, for company accounts, to your company's administrators through exports and connected services. If you or your company connect Salesforce, the contacts you collect are sent to that Salesforce account.

Company administrators can create, change, switch off and export the cards of people in their company workspace.

On your device

We set one cookie that keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share your information for advertising.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your information while your account exists. To delete one card, open it and choose Delete card. To delete everything, go to Account and choose Delete my account: your account, cards, links, collected contacts and statistics are removed from our servers.

Where it runs

MK Cards is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes and stores data on our behalf.
