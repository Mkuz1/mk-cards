MK Cards lets you create a digital business card and share it with a link or QR code.

What we store. Your account (username, email address and a scrambled form of your password that cannot be read back), the details you put on your cards (name, job title, company, bio, phone, email, website, address, links and photo), and the details visitors choose to send to a card owner (name, email, phone, company, note). We also keep a count of how often a card is opened, and for up to a day the internet address of sign-up and failed log-in attempts, to prevent abuse.

Who can see it. Anyone with a card's link or QR code can see that card. Your account email is not printed on your card unless you add it there, but the "Share your details" button on your card page opens the visitor's email app with a message addressed to it, so a visitor who uses that button will see it. A WhatsApp number you add is shown as a chat button. Details a visitor sends are visible only to the card's owner.

On your device. We set one cookie that keeps you logged in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Deleting. To delete one card, open it, choose Edit, then Delete card. To delete everything, go to Settings and choose Delete my account: your account, cards, links and the contacts sent to you are removed from our servers.

Where it runs. MK Cards is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes requests on our behalf.
